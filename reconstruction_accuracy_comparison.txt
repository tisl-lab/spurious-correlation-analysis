Notes:
- Fine-tuned worst-group accuracy is left blank — the ablation reports for the fine-tuned runs don't compute it (only the zero-shot reports do).
- MSAE reconstructs LESS faithfully as activation sparsity drops (TopKReLU_64 has fewer active latents than TopKReLU_256, same 4096-latent dictionary): its zero-shot swing grows from -3.4% to +4.8% and fine-tuned from -1.2% to -3.5% — both moving further from the 0%-delta ideal.
- RouteSAE shows the same direction but far more mildly, and in the opposite direction to MSAE: K=256 (more active concepts) is closer to a no-op than K=32 in both CLIP modes.

RouteSAE's group-level swings stay under 4 points everywhere in this table.
